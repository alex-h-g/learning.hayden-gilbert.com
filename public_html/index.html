Geometry · Grades 7–10

Basic Geometry

Learn the formulas for area, perimeter, circumference, volume and surface area. Match shapes to formulas in Pick a Pair, see why each wrong answer was wrong, and revisit the ones you find hardest.

  • 3 levels and 6 practice modes

  • Free printable cheat sheet

How these games work

🎯

Learn from mistakes

The games show you the right answer when you slip up, so you understand it rather than just guessing.

🔁

Practise what’s hard

The things you miss come back more often, so practice time goes where it’s needed most.

🔒

Private by design

No accounts, ads or tracking. Progress is kept only in your browser on this device. Basic Geometry and the Arithmetic Gang have Reset progress buttons for shared computers.
